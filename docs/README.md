# OctoAcme Project Management Documentation

## Overview

OctoAcme uses a structured, iterative approach to project management that emphasizes customer value, clear ownership, data-driven decisions, and psychological safety. Our processes guide teams through every phase of project delivery—from initiation through retrospective and continuous improvement.

OctoAcme operates a **lifecycle-based project management framework** consisting of five core phases: Initiation, Planning, Execution, Release, and Retrospective & Continuous Improvement. During Initiation, teams validate business need and stakeholder alignment through a lightweight One-pager that captures the problem statement, success metrics, and resource requirements. Once approved, the Planning phase breaks work into prioritized, estimated backlog items with clear acceptance criteria and dependencies. Execution follows a rhythm of daily standups, weekly delivery syncs, and regular demos, with work tracked on a project board (Backlog → Ready → In Progress → In Review → QA → Done). Finally, Release and Retrospective phases ensure quality and capture learnings to drive continuous improvement.

**Roles and ownership** are clearly defined across OctoAcme projects. Product Managers define what should be built and prioritize the backlog based on customer value and data-driven insights. Project Managers coordinate delivery, manage risks and dependencies, and maintain transparent communication with stakeholders. Developers implement features, write tests, and participate in planning and design reviews. QA/Testing teams validate quality and acceptance criteria. This structure ensures that each project has named ownership while enabling cross-functional collaboration around shared success metrics and outcomes.

**Communication and risk management** are woven throughout OctoAcme's execution model. Teams maintain a Risk Register that tracks identified risks by impact, likelihood, and mitigation status, with escalation paths that flow from team-level triage through the PM, Product Lead, and ultimately to project sponsors. Weekly syncs between PM and Product Manager ensure alignment on priorities and blockers, while broader stakeholder updates provide visibility into progress and decisions. Quality assurance is integrated throughout the delivery cycle through automated CI checks, clear acceptance criteria, and structured review processes, helping OctoAcme maintain high standards while supporting fast iteration.

## Core Principles

- **Customer-first**: Prioritize customer value and usability
- **Iterative delivery**: Deliver small, testable increments
- **Clear ownership**: Each project has a named PM and Product Lead
- **Data-informed decisions**: Measure impact and iterate based on evidence
- **Psychological safety**: Encourage feedback and learning
