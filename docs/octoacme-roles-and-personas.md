# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Work with **Technical Lead/Architect** for design guidance and technical strategy
- Collaborate with **QA/Testing Lead** on test plans and acceptance criteria validation
- Receive requirements from **Product Managers** and feedback from **Project Managers**
- Report technical risks to **Technical Lead/Architect** and escalate blockers to **Project Manager**

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Align with **Stakeholders/Sponsors** on business requirements and priorities
- Define success metrics for **Project Managers** and **QA/Testing Leads**
- Provide acceptance criteria to **Developers** and **Technical Lead/Architect**
- Review quality and impact metrics with **QA/Testing Lead**

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Escalate risks and blockers to **Stakeholders/Sponsors**
- Coordinate dependencies with **Technical Lead/Architect** and cross-team leads
- Track quality metrics from **QA/Testing Lead**
- Report project health to **Product Manager** and **Stakeholders/Sponsors**
- Facilitate communication between all team roles

---

## QA/Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA/Testing Leads define quality standards, create comprehensive test plans, and validate that deliverables meet acceptance criteria and quality gates before release.

### Responsibilities
- Create and execute test plans and QA strategies
- Conduct manual and automated testing across features
- Validate acceptance criteria compliance
- Report quality metrics and risks to the team
- Identify and document defects with reproducible steps
- Work with Developers to ensure testability and adequate test coverage
- Define quality gates and acceptance thresholds

### Goals
- Ensure product quality and customer satisfaction
- Reduce production defects and rework
- Maintain customer trust and brand reputation
- Enable faster, more confident releases

### Typical Communication
- Quality reviews and test result reports
- Acceptance criteria validation sessions with Developers
- Defect reports and quality metrics dashboards
- Risk assessments on quality-related issues

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Review acceptance criteria with **Product Managers** to understand quality expectations
- Collaborate with **Developers** on test coverage and testable design
- Report quality metrics and risks to **Project Manager**
- Consult with **Technical Lead/Architect** on technical test strategies
- Work with **Security Lead** on security and compliance testing

---

## Stakeholder / Sponsor

### Role Summary
Stakeholders and Sponsors provide business context, approve strategic initiatives, remove organizational blockers, and receive project status updates. They are the voice of business priorities and resource constraints.

### Responsibilities
- Define business requirements and success metrics
- Approve project initiation, scope changes, and major decisions
- Remove organizational blockers and provide resource approval
- Provide budget and capacity allocation
- Receive and review project status and milestone updates
- Escalate business-level risks and trade-offs

### Goals
- Ensure projects deliver measurable business value
- Maintain stakeholder alignment and executive visibility
- Minimize business risk and resource waste
- Enable smooth cross-organizational coordination

### Typical Communication
- Milestone reviews and executive summaries
- Monthly/quarterly status reports
- Approval gates and decision checkpoints
- Escalation resolution and blocker removal

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Align with **Product Manager** on business requirements and success metrics
- Receive project status from **Project Manager**
- Approve major scope and timeline changes proposed by **Project Manager**
- Review business outcomes with **Product Manager** and **QA/Testing Lead**
- Provide guidance and unblock issues for all team members

---

## Technical Lead / Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Leads and Architects define technical strategy, review design decisions, identify technical risks, and ensure system reliability, maintainability, and scalability.

### Responsibilities
- Design system architecture and technical approach
- Review technical designs and pull requests for quality and standards
- Identify technical debt, risks, and performance concerns
- Mentor Developers on technical standards and best practices
- Ensure systems meet scalability, security, and reliability requirements
- Coordinate with Security Lead on security architecture
- Propose technical mitigations for risks

### Goals
- Ensure scalability and long-term maintainability of systems
- Reduce technical risk and prevent architectural debt
- Enable team productivity through clear technical guidance
- Maintain high code quality and system reliability

### Typical Communication
- Technical design reviews and architecture documentation
- Code review feedback and guidance
- Technical risk assessments
- Architecture decision records (ADRs)

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Provide technical guidance to **Developers** on design and implementation
- Review requirements with **Product Manager** for technical feasibility
- Report technical risks to **Project Manager**
- Collaborate with **QA/Testing Lead** on testing strategies
- Work with **Security Lead** on security architecture and compliance

---

## Security Lead

### Role Summary
Security Leads ensure security requirements are met throughout the project lifecycle, review for vulnerabilities and compliance gaps, and manage security incidents and threat response.

### Responsibilities
- Define security requirements and compliance standards
- Review designs and code for security vulnerabilities
- Manage security scanning, testing, and compliance verification
- Lead incident response for security issues
- Provide security training and awareness guidance
- Coordinate with external compliance and audit teams
- Identify and assess security risks

### Goals
- Protect customer data and organizational assets
- Reduce security risk and compliance violations
- Maintain customer trust and regulatory compliance
- Enable secure product development practices

### Typical Communication
- Security requirements documentation
- Vulnerability reports and remediation plans
- Incident coordination and post-mortems
- Security training and compliance updates

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Define security requirements with **Product Manager** and **Stakeholders/Sponsors**
- Review designs with **Technical Lead/Architect** on security architecture
- Review code and implementations with **Developers** for vulnerabilities
- Report security risks to **Project Manager** for escalation
- Coordinate security testing with **QA/Testing Lead**
